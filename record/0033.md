# Record 0033

The vendored kernel is retired. The demolition date record/0020 set has arrived.

`lean/Vps/` is gone. The engine now arrives as a pinned lake dependency
(`lean/lake-manifest.json` → `../../vibe-proof-system/kernel`), so there is one copy of the
constitutional text, upstream, and nothing here to drift. `lean/Vps/DIGESTS.json` and the
doctor check that compared against it are deleted rather than maintained, which is what
naming a watcher as debt is for. Doctor now asserts the simpler and stronger fact: the
kernel is a dependency and there is no second copy.

What made this possible is upstream record/0005 — the sovereign digest became a parameter.
Before it, a jurisdiction could obtain its own genesis only by editing the shared engine, so
vendoring was not a choice but a consequence. This repository now supplies its own digest in
its generated book, computed from `law/genesis.md` rather than typed, and the 29-instrument
book re-compiled against the dependency unchanged.

## A statute lost its subject, and could not be repointed

Moving the kernel out left [2026] VPS 2, the Kernel Protection Act, guarding `lean/Vps/` —
a path that no longer exists. The natural remedy is to supersede it with the same protection
over the new location. **That is impossible, by proof.** VPS 2 is entrenched, and
`entrenched_immune` holds that an entrenched instrument cannot be superseded at all. The
kernel will not compile a book that tries.

So VPS 2 stands, and stands vacuous. [2026] VPS 30, the Book Protection Act, is enacted
alongside it over `lean/Vjs/`, where the generated statute book now lives — verified to deny
a hand edit and to allow the same edit with a record.

This is the price of entrenchment and it is worth stating plainly, because it will recur:
entrenchment buys immunity from a machinery that might soften itself, and it pays for that
with the inability to follow its own subject when the subject moves. An entrenched statute
can be supplemented but never repointed. Choosing entrenchment is therefore a bet that the
thing being protected will not move — and when the bet loses, the record grows a dead
instrument rather than a wrong one. Given the alternative is law that can quietly retarget
itself, that is the right trade, but it should be made knowingly.
