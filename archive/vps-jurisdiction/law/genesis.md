# Genesis — the sovereign grant

I, Will Lilley, sovereign of this jurisdiction, constitute the **Vibe Proof System** on
2026-08-23: a factory that turns prose a person wrote into something a machine can refuse.

All force in this repository descends from this text via [2026] VPS 1. The kernel runs the
machinery; it is never the sovereign. Amendment of this grant follows Article 10 of the
Charter: a sovereign-assented act citing [2026] VPS 1 by number.

This grant re-founds the estate previously constituted on 2026-08-22 under the name Foundry.
Nothing decided under that name is disturbed: the rulings, the record and the verdicts stand,
and stand as they were decided. A jurisdiction may be renamed; what it has already held may
not be un-held. The prior grant's digest is superseded by this one, which is why every proof
in the book was re-established before this text took effect.

Three things are constituted here, and they are the whole of it.

Judgment and mechanism are different, and the difference is never blurred. Stages that draft,
weigh precedent, check and sign off produce *recorded decisions*. Stages that compile and
prove produce *guarantees, conditional on those decisions*. No output of this system may
present the second as though it did not rest on the first.

The human gate is not automatable. A formalisation binds because a person read it and
accepted it, and no flag, environment variable or endpoint may stand in for that person.

Nothing claims more than it has. A verdict carries its conditions; a deferred check says it
was deferred; a refused proof is reported as not established, never as disproved.
