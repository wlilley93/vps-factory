# SPEC-LAW (local commentary; the operative mechanics are the kernel's — §21)

Spec is law: the signed-off formalization is the case's governing text.
Rulings are precedent: a modelling question answered once binds every later draft.
The kernel is clerk, not court: it applies law mechanically; benches decide semantics.
Every denial names its instrument; every sign-off files a ruling.
An unfalsifiable rule is unenactable; every operative statute carries a deny and an allow vector.
